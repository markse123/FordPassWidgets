03 / GOOD TO KNOW

The useful details.

Complete guide ↗
Does FordPassWidgets collect my data?+

Runs locally. No project data collection. FordPassWidgets has no developer-operated backend, analytics or tracking. Your account details, vehicle data and reports are not automatically sent to the project or its maintainers. This site has no Ford sign-in form or vehicle telemetry.

Scriptable stores settings, tokens and cached readings on your device. Sign-in and vehicle requests go directly to Ford and its service providers; optional location/weather features use the documented services. The installer downloads public code from GitHub. Passwords are not saved; tokens use Scriptable’s shared Keychain, accessible to other installed scripts. Only install scripts you trust. Read the privacy notes for caches, optional location/weather and removal.

Will it work with my Ford?+

The design covers common FordPass readings for gas, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and electric vehicles, including F-Series, Bronco and Mustang families. That is an intended scope, not verification on every model. Ford’s available data, modem/services and account determine what appears. Review model support and testing limits before relying on a feature. US sign-in is the initial focus; other regions are unverified.

How do multiple vehicles work?+

Connect one Ford account and choose vehicles from its garage, with a manual fallback. Each has its own data and settings. Vehicles changes the app view; Make default vehicle sets the default for unbound widgets. Use Copy widget parameter for a fixed vehicle. Browsing another vehicle does not move that bound widget.

What EV features are included?+

Read-only traction-battery percentage, electric range, plug and charging state, where Ford supplies recognized fields. Traction charge is separate from 12V battery status. Live EV variants still need testing. This release does not add EV climate/conditioning, start/stop charging, charge limits or an unverified completion ETA.

Which Pro Power systems were tested?+

The predecessor project tested one US 7.2 kW F-150 PowerBoost, including A and B under load together. A was compared at two loads; total matched the circuit sum. Watt/capacity estimates are restricted to an HEV profile reporting the known raw 7200 capacity. Other variants, including BEVs, show mapping-unverified/raw details. One raw unit ≈ one watt is only corroborated on that tested system. Separate B calibration, physical outlet mapping, other ratings and regions remain unverified. This new app still needs live regression testing.

Enable Pro Power per vehicle and request a status reading. Estimates are last reported, with unverified sample time. Missing data shows —. Use the vehicle’s own controls for outlets and Generator Mode; the app does not switch them. Pro Power instructions.

Does Refresh fetch everything immediately?+

Enable Wake / refresh for that vehicle to show Refresh; otherwise the widget offers a passive Fetch. Refresh opens Scriptable for confirmation, requests permitted vehicle status and optional Pro Power status, then reads available cloud data. Keep the app open while checks finish; the flow can take around a minute. Cooldowns/backoff can skip stages. A completed request or cloud read does not prove each measurement changed, and iOS decides when the widget redraws.

Fetch telemetry reads Ford’s stored cloud data without a vehicle-status command. Pro Power’s status-result age is not its embedded sample time. Refresh and measurement times.

Can I choose metric formats and tire thresholds?+

Choose US or Metric in Units & formats, with independent Auto/PSI/kPa/bar tire pressure. Per-widget format words can override shared settings without refreshing data. Four/six-wheel handling is available for reported tire positions. Low-tire alerts start off; configure your vehicle’s front/rear thresholds rather than using a universal value.
